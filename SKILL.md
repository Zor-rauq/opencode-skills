# ADR Writing

Capture decisions clearly so future engineers understand why a path was chosen.

## ADR format

Use this structure:

- Title
- Status
- Context
- Decision
- Consequences
- Alternatives considered

## Rules

- Keep it short.
- Focus on the decision and why it was made.
- Include tradeoffs, not just benefits.
- Record constraints that shaped the decision.
- Avoid vague wording and generic architecture language.
- Name rejected alternatives explicitly when useful.

## Style

- Use direct, factual language.
- Prefer concrete impacts over abstract claims.
- Avoid long background sections.
- Do not turn ADRs into full design documents.

## Output behavior

Generate ADRs that are brief, decision-focused, and useful during future change discussions.
